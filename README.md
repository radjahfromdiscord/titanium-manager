## Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

The short version: everything the Manager knows about you stays on your PC. There is no account with us, no analytics and no tracking.

**1. What the Manager keeps on your PC**
- Your Manager login (username and password check). The password itself is never stored: it is used to unlock the encrypted vault.
- The Roblox logins you add (account cookies), encrypted with Windows' own protection and, when you use a Manager login, also with your password.
- The Database: a stored copy of every login you added, with its folder and note, its backup copies, a trash for deleted entries and a daily snapshot of the last 14 days - all encrypted the same way.
- Your notes, settings, account list, the servers your accounts were in, and the Manager's own log.
- The invite check: a small file that remembers this PC was let in, protected for your Windows user.

All of it is in the Manager's data folder (Settings has a button to open it). Deleting that folder removes it.

**2. Backup files you export**
Export Backup writes your stored logins into one file, encrypted with a password you choose, to a place you choose. The Manager never uploads it. Anyone who has the file and the password can open it, so keep both safe.

**3. What leaves your PC**
The Manager only talks to:
- Roblox (roblox.com and its API addresses): to sign your accounts in, launch and join games, and show names, pictures, account ages, presence and server details. These requests carry the logins you added, as Roblox needs them.
- GitHub (api.github.com and its download addresses): to check for and download signed updates. GitHub sees your IP address, like any website.
- Google's connection check (google.com/generate_204) and roblox.com: to tell whether your internet or VPN connection works.

Nothing is sent to the authors of the Manager. Your logins, notes and settings are never uploaded anywhere.

**4. Invite codes**
While the invite server is not online, invite codes are checked on your PC only. When it goes online, checking or creating a code will send the code and the time to that server, and this policy will be updated first to say so.

**5. Other programs**
Roblox, your executor, your VPN and GitHub have their own privacy policies; the Manager does not control what they collect.

**6. Children**
The Manager is not meant for children under 13.

**7. Changes**
If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date at the top changes.

**8. Questions**
Ask the person who gave you your invite code, or the place you got the Manager from.
